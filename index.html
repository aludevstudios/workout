100-Day Bilateral Stack

⚡ 4-Minute Pre-Game Primer

Dynamic pre-match activation for soccer & tennis to prepare ankles, adductors, and nervous system without static stretching.

Minute 1: Ankle Hops & Pogo Bounces (60s)
Pre-heats the Achilles tendons and calves without overloading the insertion.
Minute 2: Walking Lunges with Torso Twist (60s)
Activates quads and dynamically opens hip flexors evenly on both legs.
Minute 3: Lateral Shuffles to Carioca (60s)
Wakes up adductors (groin) and abductors for rapid on-court cutting.
Minute 4: Build-Up Accelerations (50% ➔ 80%) (60s)
Prepares the central nervous system for sprints so muscles do not clamp down.
